# Coffee Chain Sales & Profit Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Coffee Chain Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, profitability, product performance, market trends, and inventory.

The project transforms business data into meaningful visual insights through a **three-page interactive dashboard**, enabling users to monitor KPIs, compare business segments, identify performance variations, and explore data dynamically using slicers.

## Problem Statement

A coffee chain needs a centralized solution to monitor its sales and profitability across products, product types, markets, market sizes, states, and years.

Analyzing these business dimensions separately can make it difficult to track performance against targets, identify monthly trends, compare product profitability, and recognize high- and low-performing markets.

**Objective:** To develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates key business metrics, supports multidimensional analysis, and helps stakeholders explore performance and make data-informed decisions.

##  Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX & Measures** — KPI calculations and achievement metrics, where implemented
- **Interactive Slicers** — Dynamic filtering across dashboard visuals
- **Business Intelligence & Data Analysis** — Performance monitoring, trend analysis, and business reporting

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales & Profit Performance

Analyzes overall business performance, profitability, and sales trends.

**Key Visualizations & KPIs:**
- Total Sales and Total Profit
- Sales Achievement % and Profit Achievement %
- Monthly Sales and Profit Trends
- Profit by Product Type and Market Size
- State-wise Sales Variance and Profit Analysis

### 2. Product & Market Analysis

Evaluates product performance and sales distribution across different market segments.

**Key Visualizations:**
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Regular vs. Decaf Sales Distribution
- Sales by Product Type and Market
- Interactive filters for Year, Market, Market Size, Product Type, and Coffee Type

### 3. Performance & Operations

Examines inventory, sales achievement, and profitability across geographic and market segments.

**Key Visualizations:**
- Sales and Inventory by Product Type
- Market-wise Profit Achievement by Year
- Top 10 States by Sales Achievement %
- Bottom 10 States by Sales Achievement %
- Interactive filters for Year, Market, Product Type, State, and Product

##  Key Business Questions

The dashboard helps investigate the following questions:

1. What are the overall sales and profit performance levels?
2. How do sales and profit change over time?
3. Which products contribute the most to sales and profitability?
4. How do Regular and Decaf products compare in sales contribution?
5. How does business performance vary by market, market size, and state?
6. Which states have the highest and lowest sales achievement percentages?
7. How do sales and inventory compare across product types?
8. How does profit achievement vary across markets and years?

##  Project Outcome

The project delivers a **three-page interactive Power BI report** that brings together sales, profit, product, market, geographic, and inventory analysis.

The dashboard enables users to:

- Monitor business KPIs and achievement percentages.
- Compare product-level sales and profitability.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate market and state-level performance.
- Explore sales and profit achievement against targets.
- Interactively filter and investigate different business scenarios.

The result is a centralized reporting solution designed to support business performance monitoring, exploratory analysis, and data-driven decision-making.
